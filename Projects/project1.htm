Project 1 - Web Site Critiques - Rachael Hull

Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Jami Lin - www.jamilin.com

  1. My first impression of this site was just "wow" with bewilderment. Definitely very jarring compared to today's modern websites.
  2. The site flow is easy understand and I could see what services Jami provides with navigation links moving left to right and content read from top to bottom.
  3. Site structure is a little confusing as there were more links in navigation and four other links at the bottom in the body that mentioned the exact same services.
    Under the navigation bar, it looks like there's information that should be in a footer at the bottom of the page, like her social media links and payment methods.
  4. The site graphics and layout are kind of bizarre, as she has a blarring green image at the top and the images of Jami in the four links at the bottom
    don't really indicate exactly what the difference between "Galactic Spirit", "Chakras & ColorAlchemy" or "Reverse Aging Skin Care" are.
  5. The site itself loads fast at a reasonable rate and there wasn't any wait time to visit this website.
  6. The text color was consistent and the size was mostly readbable, however the links in the navigation were smaller and hard to read.
    The background was pure white and it didn't detract from the site.
  7. The site has a colorful navigation bar and none of the links were broken. the only inconsistency was the "Galactic Spirit" link
    in the body not matching to the page when I clicked the blue button link at the bottom titled, "Evolve YOU within Awakening Humanity."
  8. The color scheme is all over the place and none of the other pages had the same layout as the home page and went to a completely different website.
  9. The home page and the other links except for Feng Shui and Chakras were all responsive and could format the content with different screen sizes.
  10. Other things that this site offered was this very annoying pop up for "Galactic Gems" every time I navigated away from any page and went back to it.
